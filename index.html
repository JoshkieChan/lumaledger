    PILOT CHECK PLAN

    Validate the journey before claiming impact.

    Any real-world pilot should be opt-in and consented before it begins. This demo does not recruit participants, contact households, or collect results.

    Journey completion
    Share of participants who finish the calculator and mark at least one next step.
    Action progress
    Which suggested steps participants choose to mark complete.
    Self-reported outcomes
    Contact, application, or support reports, kept distinct from verified outcomes.
    Bill comparison
    Optional before/after amounts; report direction and missingness, not causal savings.
    Privacy and usability
    Track task completion and confusion with consent; target zero collection or transmission of household inputs.

    Suggested protocol: test task comprehension and completion with willing participants only after informed consent and an approved study plan. Summarize aggregate metrics; do not retain raw household bills, ZIP codes, or income.
